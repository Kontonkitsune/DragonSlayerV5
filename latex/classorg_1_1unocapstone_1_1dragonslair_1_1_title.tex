\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A \doxylink{classorg_1_1unocapstone_1_1dragonslair_1_1_title}{Title} that has the ability to be requested by a \doxylink{classorg_1_1unocapstone_1_1dragonslair_1_1_customer}{Customer}. A \doxylink{classorg_1_1unocapstone_1_1dragonslair_1_1_title}{Title} consists of a title, price, notes, a flagged property, and unique ID that is generated by the database.
